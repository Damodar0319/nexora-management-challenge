## 🌟 Overview & Concept

**NEXORA Management Challenge** is an interactive, gamified simulation created for the **Essentials of Management (EOM)** curriculum and executive leadership workshops. 

Rather than reviewing abstract management theories through traditional slides, players step into the role of **Chief Organizational Architect**. Your task is to construct a resilient, high-performing corporate enterprise from the ground up by assembling a **10-Block Management Pyramid**.

Every strategic choice directly impacts the stability, cultural vitality, and operational resilience of your company. But beware: among healthy organizational practices lurk **disguised management pitfalls and toxic anti-patterns** (such as *Micromanagement*, *Analysis Paralysis*, *Endless Red Tape*, and *Burnout Overtime*). Choosing these disguised traps will compromise the organization, penalize your score, and cause the enterprise to buckle during macroeconomic stress tests.

---

## 🎯 Game Objectives

1. **Construct the 10-Block Pyramid**:
   Fill all 10 designated architectural slots across the 4 organizational tiers (Foundation, Core Engine, Strategic Bridge, and Crown Peak).
2. **Discern Healthy Practices from Anti-Patterns**:
   Identify and select genuine, value-creating management attributes while avoiding deceptive traps disguised as corporate rigor.
3. **Achieve a Master S+ Grade (100 Points)**:
   Maximize your score by matching block color domains with slot target attributes and achieving a zero-pitfall placement.
4. **Survive the Market Earthquake Stress Test**:
   Achieve **100% Earthquake Resistance** by reinforcing Tier 1 (Strong Foundation) with sound operational systems, continuous technological modernization, and genuine employee care.

## 🎨 The 4 Core Management Pillars (Color Codes)

The simulation organizes modern organizational theory into **4 primary color-coded domains**, each representing 5 strategic management practices (3 healthy principles and 2 disguised anti-patterns):

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        NEXORA 4-PILLAR ECOSYSTEM                       │
├─────────────────┬─────────────────┬──────────────────┬─────────────────┤
│  🔴 RED         │  🟡 YELLOW      │  🔵 BLUE         │  🟢 GREEN       │
│  Leadership &   │  Innovation &   │  Operations &    │  People &       │
│  Direction      │  Agility        │  Systems         │  Culture        │
└─────────────────┴─────────────────┴──────────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

### 🔴 Red: Leadership & Direction
* **Strategic Focus**: Transformational vision, clear goal cascading, crisis decision-making, and organizational alignment.
* **Academic Backing**: Peter Drucker's *Management by Objectives (MBO / OKRs)*, Bernard Bass's *Transformational Leadership*, and Contingency Theory.
* **Healthy Attributes**: Inspirational vision, cross-functional synergy, and courageous executive execution.
* **Toxic Traps**: Micromanagement and departmental silos that throttle autonomy and divide units.

### 🟡 Yellow: Innovation & Agility
* **Strategic Focus**: Creative ideation, rapid experimentation, AI & modern digital automation, and nimble iteration.
* **Academic Backing**: Eric Ries's *Lean Startup* (Build-Measure-Learn), Tim Brown's *Design Thinking*, and Industry 4.0 automation.
* **Healthy Attributes**: Dedicated innovation sandboxes, cutting-edge AI tooling, and 24-hour customer prototype cycles.
* **Toxic Traps**: Analysis paralysis (endless committee debates) and stubborn resistance to new digital tools.

### 🔵 Blue: Operations & Systems
* **Strategic Focus**: Streamlined workflows, rigorous quality assurance, evidence-based telemetry, and scalable governance.
* **Academic Backing**: W. Edwards Deming's *Total Quality Management (TQM)*, Six Sigma, and Jeffrey Pfeffer's *Evidence-Based Management*.
* **Healthy Attributes**: Frictionless operational handoffs, zero-defect quality control, and data-driven governance.
* **Toxic Traps**: Dysfunctional bureaucratic red tape and reckless corner-cutting on safety/compliance.

### 🟢 Green: People & Culture
* **Strategic Focus**: Psychological safety, continuous human capital development, employee well-being, and work-life balance.
* **Academic Backing**: Amy Edmondson's *Psychological Safety*, Gary Becker's *Human Capital Theory*, and Frederick Herzberg's *Motivation-Hygiene Theory*.
* **Healthy Attributes**: Safe-to-fail trust environments, funded career growth academies, and proactive burnout prevention.
* **Toxic Traps**: Fear-based blame culture and exploitative 70-hour burnout overtime.

## 🎮 How to Play — Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Flexible Placement Controls
NEXORA supports **three intuitive interaction modes** to fit every user preference:
* **Drag & Drop**: Click and hold any block card from the bottom palette, drag it upward, and drop it onto an empty slot on the canvas.
* **Top-Down Slot Picker**: Click any empty slot directly on the pyramid. A clean modal selector opens, displaying recommended blocks filtered for that slot's target attribute.
* **Click-to-Place / Quick Snap**: Click a block in the bottom palette to select it, then click your desired slot. Alternatively, click the quick-snap button on any card to automatically snap it into the first available matching slot.

### 2. Single-Use Inventory Rule
* To ensure realistic resource allocation, **once a block is placed into a slot, it automatically disappears from the available palette** so it cannot be duplicated.
* Need to adjust your structure? **Click any placed block on the pyramid** to instantly remove it and return it to your available palette.

### 3. Real-Time Search & Color Filtering
* Use the color filter tabs (`All`, `🔴 Red`, `🟡 Yellow`, `🔵 Blue`, `🟢 Green`) in the palette to narrow down choices by domain.
* Type into the real-time **Search Bar** to instantly find blocks by principle (e.g., *"Psychological Safety"*, *"Drucker"*, *"AI"*, *"Lean"*).

### 4. Custom Block Studio
* Want to introduce your own management theory into the game? Click **"+ Custom Block"** in the palette header.
* Configure a custom block title, subtitle, color domain, Lucide icon, point value, and theoretical management principle. Custom blocks immediately become playable!

### 5. Instant 1-Click Auto Fill Demo
* Delivering an in-class presentation or executive walkthrough? Click the **"⚡ 1-Click Auto Fill"** button in the top banner.
* The system instantly populates the 10 slots with the optimal, non-pitfall management attributes and triggers the evaluation modal for demonstration.

## 📊 Scoring Formula, Grading Scale & Stress Test

### Scoring Math
When evaluating your structure (either automatically upon filling 10 slots or manually via **"Check Result & Finish"**):

$$\text{Final Score} = \text{Points Added} - \text{Points Deducted}$$

* **Correct Block Base**: $+10\text{ points}$
* **Target Color Match Bonus**: $+2\text{ bonus points}$ (awarded when block color matches the slot's intended attribute)
* **Wrong Answer Penalty**: $-15\text{ penalty points}$ per pitfall selected
* **Master Perfection Bonus**: When all 10 slots are filled with **0 wrong answers**, raw score is automatically rounded to a perfect **$100 / 100$**.

### Grading Scale

| Grade | Title | Criteria | Evaluation Outcome |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🏆 **S+** | **High-Performance Master Monument** | 100 Points (10/10 Correct, 0 Pitfalls) | Flawless organizational synergy. Triggers celebratory confetti and fanfare! |
| 🥇 **A** | **Resilient Enterprise** | 80 – 99 Points (Max 1 Pitfall) | Strong organizational health with minor isolated vulnerabilities. |
| 🥈 **B** | **Developing Organization** | 60 – 79 Points (1 Pitfall) | Functional operations, but compromised by strategic frictions. |
| 🥉 **C** | **Fragile Organization** | < 60 Points or 2 Pitfalls | Significant structural gaps; vulnerable to market disruptions. |
| 💥 **FAIL** | **Critical Organizational Collapse** | 3+ Pitfalls Selected | Fatal systemic dysfunction; leadership and operational failure. |

### 🌋 Earthquake Resistance Formula & Simulation

In business, organizational design is tested by macro crises (recessions, disruption, supply shocks). The **Earthquake Resistance Metric (0 – 100%)** mathematically simulates this resilience:

$$\text{Resistance} = (\text{Base Ratio} \times 50) + (\text{Completion Ratio} \times 30) + (\text{Correct Count} \times 2) - (\text{Wrong Count} \times 25)$$

* **Base Ratio (50% weight)**: Measures how many Tier 1 Foundation slots are filled with healthy, non-pitfall blocks.
* **Completion Ratio (30% weight)**: How close the structure is to 10 filled slots.
* **Correct Count (20% weight)**: Direct contribution of validated principles.
* **Pitfall Deduction**: $-25\%$ resistance penalty for each wrong answer!

> **Interactive Earthquake Simulator**: In the Victory/Results modal, click **"🌋 Test Earthquake Resistance"** to trigger a seismic stress test! The canvas vibrates with authentic screen-shake animations and seismic low-frequency audio rumble to visually demonstrate structural stability.

## ✨ Special Features & Polish

* 🔊 **Zero-Dependency Web Audio Synthesizer**: Uses native browser Web Audio API to generate custom sound effects (block snap, remove pop, victory fanfare, warning buzzer, seismic rumble). No external audio files or bandwidth required; includes an instant mute toggle in the navbar.
* 🎊 **Celebratory Particle Physics**: Integrated with `canvas-confetti` to fire celebratory multi-angle particle bursts when achieving an S+ grade.
* 🛡️ **Zero Spoilers During Assembly**: Disguised blocks display uniform power ratings and clean aesthetics before evaluation, preventing players from guessing based on visual giveaways.
* 📱 **Fully Responsive Layout**: Built with a responsive CSS grid, touch-friendly drag targets, and dynamic drawer scrolling that works across mobile, tablet, and wide-screen projector displays.

## 🚀 Getting Started & Local Setup

### Prerequisites
* **Node.js**: version `18.x` or higher
* **npm**: version `9.x` or higher

### Installation Steps

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Damodar0319/nexora-management-challenge.git
   cd nexora-management-challenge
   ```

2. **Install project dependencies**:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Start the local development server**:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   Open your browser and navigate to `http://localhost:5173`.

4. **Build for production**:
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```
   The compiled production bundle will be generated in the `dist/` directory.

5. **Preview production build locally**:
   ```bash
   npm run preview
   ```

## 🎓 Presentation & Classroom Guide (EOM Presentation)

If you are presenting this project for an **Essentials of Management (EOM)** evaluation or class demonstration, use the following recommended presentation flow:

1. **The Hook (1 Min)**:
   * Introduce the problem: Management theory often feels disconnected from real decisions.
   * Introduce NEXORA: *"We engineered an interactive management architecture simulator where students and leaders experience the consequences of organizational choices firsthand."*
2. **The Architecture (2 Mins)**:
   * Walk through the **4 Pillars**: Red (Leadership), Yellow (Innovation), Blue (Operations), Green (People).
   * Explain the **Pyramid Hierarchy**: Why operational and cultural stability at Tier 1 (Foundation) is essential to support Tier 4 (Apex Leadership).
3. **Live Interactive Gameplay Demo (3 Mins)**:
   * Demonstrate **Drag-and-Drop** or **Slot Picker** placement.
   * Intentionally pick a toxic anti-pattern (e.g., *Micromanagement* or *Burnout Overtime*) alongside healthy blocks to show realistic team discussions.
   * Click **"Check Result & Finish"** to showcase the **Results Breakdown**: Explain why the anti-pattern triggered a -15 point penalty and reduced stability.
4. **The Stress Test (1 Min)**:
   * Trigger the **Earthquake Simulation** in the modal. Explain how foundation weaknesses cause organizational collapse under stress.
5. **Classroom Auto-Fill Demonstration**:
   * Click **"1-Click Auto Fill"** to demonstrate the flawless S+ (100-Point) Master Monument with zero pitfalls and celebrate with confetti.
